PROJECT OVERVIEW:
-----------------
EcoTrack India is a smart sustainability platform designed specifically for
Indian households and citizens. It empowers users to:
1. Track everyday eco-friendly habits (wet/dry waste segregation, bucket baths,
   jute bags for sabzi mandi, short-trip walking/cycling).
2. Calculate their estimated carbon footprint tailored to Indian lifestyle data
   (CEA Indian electricity grid baseline, ARAI vehicle factors, LPG cylinder usage).
3. Complete daily and weekly sustainability challenges with duplicate-reward prevention.
4. Unlock achievement badges and monitor environmental savings (CO2 kg saved, water saved,
   plastic items avoided).
5. Learn practical sustainability tips through an Indian context Eco Guide.
